FAQ

Common questions

What if the battery dies?
You get low-battery alerts on the lock and (on Wi-Fi models) in the app. Even if it empties, two backups remain: momentary power from a power bank over USB (on models listing it), and the mechanical key. Being locked out is designed out.
Can any carpenter install a Golens lock?
Yes — for wooden doors it's a standard fitting job with the included template and hardware. For glass doors, the glass models fit without drilling, though we recommend expert installation. Video-call guidance is free either way.
Does face recognition work at night?
On models with 3D face recognition, the mapping is built to work in low light. Our team can demo it live on a video call before you buy.
How do I enrol my family's fingerprints and faces?
The lock's menu guides you step by step, and the app manages users on Wi-Fi models. If you choose expert installation, the technician enrolls everyone during the walkthrough.
What is covered under warranty?
Manufacturing defects in the mechanism, sensors and panels for 2 years from purchase — claimed directly with Golens via 1800-123-7255 or care@golens.in with your order ID.
Can I still use a normal key?
Yes — every Golens lock keeps a mechanical key as the final backup, so you're never locked out even with zero battery.
