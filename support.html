Installation

Two honest paths — both end with the same lock, working, on your door.

Expert paid installation

Answers

Everything people ask support.

What number do I call for help?

Toll-free 1800-123-7255 (9:00–17:00, every day), WhatsApp +91 99829 87865 (chats and voice, 10:30–18:00), or email support@golens.in. For choosing a lock before you buy, care@golens.in.

Do I need Wi-Fi for the lock to work?

No. Locking, fingerprint, PIN, card and key all work offline. Wi-Fi adds the remote layer: app control, notifications, remote unlock and OTP sharing. On locks like the X95, Wi-Fi is built in — no extra gateway to buy.

What happens in a power cut?

Nothing — locks run on their own batteries. A power cut doesn't touch your door. When batteries run low you get alerts (on Wi-Fi models), and USB emergency power plus a mechanical key are always there as backups.

Can Golens locks work with Alexa, Google Home or Apple Home?

The Golens Protector (X57) lists support for Alexa, Google and Apple ecosystems in its official listing. Most other Golens Wi-Fi locks use the Tuya / Smart Life app system. Always check the specific product's listing — we never imply a model supports what it doesn't.

How many people can use one lock?

Generously many — e.g. the X95 holds up to 150 fingerprints; other models state 50–100+ users. Family, staff and guests can each have their own method, and every entry is recorded.

Can I return a lock if it doesn't fit?

Golens publishes a return policy — read it before ordering at golens.in/pages/return-policy. Our strong preference is to get fit right before you order, which is why the compatibility checker and photo confirmation exist.

Do you deliver everywhere in India?

Shipping is covered by the Golens shipping policy (golens.in/pages/shipping-policy) — check it for your pin code's terms. Installation quotes are location-dependent and confirmed before scheduling.

Where are the manuals and app links?

Each product page on golens.in carries the manual and the companion app details (Tuya / Smart Life for most Wi-Fi and Bluetooth models). Support can send them on WhatsApp within minutes.
