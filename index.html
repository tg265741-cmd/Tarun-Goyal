09 — Quick answers

Before you ask.

Do Golens locks work if the power or Wi-Fi goes out?

Yes. Golens locks run on their own batteries, and every smart lock keeps a mechanical key backup. On models like the X95 you can also revive a flat lock momentarily with a power bank via USB. Wi-Fi features (remote unlock, notifications) naturally need Wi-Fi — the door itself stays lockable and unlockable by fingerprint, PIN, card and key.

Will it fit my door?

Most wooden and metal main doors between roughly 35–120 mm thick take a standard Golens lock (the SCANXPRO explicitly covers 30–120 mm). Glass doors need a glass-specific model for 8–12 mm tempered glass. Use our compatibility checker or send a photo of your door on WhatsApp — we'll confirm before you buy.

Who installs it, and what does it cost?

Two honest options: Golens can arrange expert paid installation (charges start around ₹1,200–1,500 depending on your location) with complimentary on-site support afterwards, or any local carpenter can fit it using the included template and hardware. Nothing structural is needed for standard wooden doors.

What warranty do I get?

Golens products carry a 2-year standard warranty (a few value models list 1 year — shown on each product page). Because you buy directly from Golens, warranty claims come straight to us: toll-free 1800-123-7255, care@golens.in or WhatsApp.

Can I let guests in when I'm not home?

On Wi-Fi models, yes — the app supports remote unlocking, one-time passwords and temporary passwords (model-dependent, e.g. X95). Non-Wi-Fi models keep access local: fingerprint, PIN, card and key. Every model page states exactly which methods it supports.
